# Security Policy

If you find a security bug (such as XSS in media title rendering, secret exposure in client bundles, or open redirects):

1. **Email**: Contact **frerox.toolz@gmail.com** with subject `[SECURITY] toolz-downloadz`.
2. **Reproduction**: Include steps or PoC.
3. **Safe Harbor**: Responsible reports submitted in good faith will not face legal retaliation.
